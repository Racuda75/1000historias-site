Welcome to 1000 Historias

At 1000 Historias, wine is much more than a drink. It is a memory in a bottle, a story waiting to be shared.

Guided by award-winning winemaker Julia Halupczok, you’ll be part of an intimate journey of creation: from selecting the grapes and the barrel, to the shape of the bottle and the label design.

Each barrel becomes a unique vintage, yielding around 250 exclusive bottles. It is not mass production. It is not on any shelf. It is a wine entirely your own.

Whether to celebrate a wedding, honor a legacy, or give a gift that says more than a thousand words, every sip will carry your story.

Your guide

Julia Halupczok brings more than two decades of award-winning winemaking experience.

Her philosophy is simple: wine should be personal, soulful, and beautifully crafted. She will guide you through every choice: the grape variety, the oak that will shape the barrel, the texture of the label.

With her, the process becomes as memorable as the wine itself.

The enchanted region

Your story, written in the land of Mendoza

Your story unfolds in Mendoza, Argentina, a land where sun-drenched vineyards meet the Andes mountain range. Here, altitude, soil, and climate come together to create wines of exceptional character.

The same land that inspires some of the world’s finest harvests now becomes the canvas for your creation. Rustic beauty, fresh mountain air, and infinite horizons—this is where your wine begins.

The Mendoza region is home to more than 75% of Argentina’s vineyards and is recognized for its production of Cabernet Sauvignon, Cabernet Franc, Syrah, Malbec, Chardonnay, and Bonarda grapes.
